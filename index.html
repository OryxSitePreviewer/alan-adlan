About the clinic

Dental care for the Bangi community

Klinik Pergigian Alan Adlan sits on Jalan 6/7b in Seksyen 1, a short drive from most of Bandar Baru Bangi. Our dental team treats families from around the neighbourhood, from young children having their first check-up to adults who have put off a visit for longer than they would like to admit.

The clinic was founded by our Principal Dentist, Dr. Mohd Rusman Adlan B. A. Rahman, who works alongside a team of resident dentists across general, orthodontic and restorative care. Many of the people we see were sent by a neighbour, a colleague or a family member who came before them.

We keep the clinic open from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM every day of the week, because dental problems rarely wait for a weekday afternoon. Evening and weekend appointments are available for people who work outside Bangi.

Before any treatment starts, your dentist will walk you through what is happening in your mouth, what the options are, and what each one involves. If something can reasonably wait, we will tell you. If you are nervous about the chair, say so when you arrive and we will take it slowly.

What we do

Our dental services

Routine care and longer-term treatment under one roof, so you are not sent across the Klang Valley for the next step.

General Dentistry

Check-ups, fillings, extractions and the routine care that keeps small problems from turning into big ones.

Braces & Orthodontics

Straightening crowded or uneven teeth over time, with regular adjustment visits and a plan agreed up front.

Dental Implants

Replacing a missing tooth with a fixed post and crown, so you can chew and speak without a gap.

Teeth Whitening

Lightening teeth that have stained from coffee, tea or age, after a check that your gums are healthy enough for it.

Crown & Bridge

Rebuilding a broken or heavily filled tooth, or bridging a gap using the healthy teeth on either side.

Scaling & Polishing

Clearing away the hardened plaque a toothbrush cannot reach, which is usually where bleeding gums start.

We also treat

  • Endodontics, or root canal treatment
  • Periodontics, or gum treatment
  • Oral surgery
  • Dentures and dental prostheses
  • Dental aesthetics, including veneers
  • Preventive dentistry for adults and children
